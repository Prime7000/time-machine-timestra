ABOUT THE APP

Your data. Your window. Your forecast.

Start with a CSV containing a numeric series. Choose the value to predict, select a history window by row number or date, and set how many steps you want to forecast. Expand Multivariable forecast to optionally enable other numeric columns as historical helpers. TimeS forcast estimates what follows your selected window.

▦

Choose your history

Work with the full series or focus on a range. Row numbers start at 1, and both ends of your selection are included.

↗

Explore every detail

Zoom, pan, and inspect the animated chart. Focus on the forecast or compare it with later observations already in your dataset.

↧

Take your results with you

View predictions and their bounds in a table, then download them as a CSV. Use light or dark mode while you work.

Reading your forecast

Last observed is the final actual value in your selected history. Next prediction is the median forecast for the first step after it. A step follows your data’s sampling interval: for daily data, 24 steps means 24 days.

The shaded band runs from the forecast’s 10th to 90th percentile. Forecasts are estimates, and the band describes their uncertainty. Future dates are inferred from regular history; irregular series use step labels.

ⓘ Uploads are processed by the app’s backend. Each new CSV replaces the previous upload; Clear removes the stored upload.
